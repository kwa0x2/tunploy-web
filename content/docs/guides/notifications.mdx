---
title: Email notifications
description: Get an email when servers go down, devices hit their limit or sign-ins fail.
---

Under **Settings → Notifications**, give the panel an SMTP account and it emails you when servers go down or come back, devices are added, a device uses up its data, someone fails to sign in, and so on; you choose which.

Events that happen close together arrive as one email, and at most 30 emails go out an hour.

<PanelShot name="notifications" alt="Settings → Notifications with an SMTP account and chosen events" />

## SMTP settings

Any provider that offers SMTP works:

- Use the address you send from as the **username**.
- Use port **587** with **STARTTLS**, or **465** with **TLS**.
- Set **From** to an address the account may send as.

<Callout title="Gmail and Outlook">
  They need an app password rather than your normal one.
</Callout>

**Send test email** tries the form as it is, before you save, and shows the mail server's own answer if it fails.
