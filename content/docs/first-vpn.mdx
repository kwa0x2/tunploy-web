## Sign in

Open `http://YOUR_SERVER_IP:3000` and sign in with the account you created during the install. The panel is public, but there is no sign-up page: only the account made on the server can get in.

<Callout type="warn" title="Plain HTTP at first">
  Out of the box the panel serves plain HTTP, so your password and session travel unencrypted. Settings shows a warning while that is the case. Fix it with one of the options under [HTTPS](/docs/guides/https).
</Callout>

## Deploy a server and connect

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Servers → New server**, give it a name and press **Deploy**.
</Step>
<Step>
Open the server and choose **Add peer**. Scan the QR code with the WireGuard app on your phone, or download the `.conf` file for a laptop.
</Step>
<Step>
Connect. The peer shows up as **Online** within a few seconds.
</Step>
</Steps>

<PanelShot name="qr" alt="A device's QR code, ready to scan with the WireGuard app" />

The endpoint that clients connect to comes from **Settings → General**. If the install script detected the wrong address, change it there before creating servers. Existing servers keep their own endpoint, which you can change under each server's settings.

## Manage devices

Each server's page lists its peers with their address, status, location and traffic this month.

<PanelShot name="server-detail" alt="A server's peers with their status, location and traffic" />

From there you can turn a device off, rename it, show its QR code again, see its daily and monthly usage, or give it a **monthly data limit** or an **expiry date**. A device that reaches its limit or its expiry date is blocked until the next month or until you change it, and the [activity log](#activity) records it.

## Activity

**Activity** shows who connected from where, and what changed on the panel, for the last 90 days. Filter by connections, changes or sign-ins.
